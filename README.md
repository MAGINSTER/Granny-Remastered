# Granny-Remastered
Granny Remaster is based on the game Granny and largely reworked to suit its own style and capabilities.
The map has two endings: escaping through the door and dying. To escape through the door, you need to find various items around the map, such as keys, password sheets, crowbars, etc. If you make too much noise, Granny will hear you and come to you, possibly setting a trap. One to two players can play. Granny is played by a bot.
